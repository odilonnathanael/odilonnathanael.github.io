This week, I added a "Projects" section to my site to showcase my personal work.

## Why a projects page?

- Present your work in a structured way
- Show your technical skills
- Facilitate recruiting or collaborations

## Structure of a typical project

1. Project name: catchy title
2. Description: goal, context, challenges
3. Technologies: stack used
4. Link: to the GitHub repository or demo

Each project is an opportunity to learn and demonstrate your skills.
