Bibliography management is essential for any research work.

## Recommended tools

### Zotero

- Free and open source
- Browser extension
- Cloud synchronization
- Export to BibTeX, Word, etc.

### Mendeley

- Free (with limits)
- Academic social network
- Personalized article recommendations

### BibTeX

- Standard format for LaTeX
- .bib files
- Used by Google Scholar, arXiv, etc.

## Example BibTeX entry

@article{nathanael2024,
  author = {Nathanael, O.},
  title = {Article title},
  journal = {Journal name},
  year = {2024},
  volume = {15},
  pages = {10-25}
}

Good bibliography management makes writing and citing sources easier.
