This week, I made my site accessible in two languages: French and English.

## Why a bilingual site?

- Reach a wider audience
- Improve international visibility
- Facilitate collaborations

## Method used

I used the Jekyll Polyglot plugin to manage multiple languages easily.

## File structure

- _posts/fr/ : articles in French
- _posts/en/ : articles in English
- Pages: about.fr.md, about.en.md, etc.

## Challenges

- Faithful content translation
- Managing links between versions
- Plugin configuration

The result is a professional site accessible to an international audience.
