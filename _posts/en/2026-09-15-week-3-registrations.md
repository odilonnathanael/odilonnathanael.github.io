This week, I registered on the main academic platforms and added links to my profiles on my site.

## Platforms

- eLibrary: Russian scientific publications database
- Google Scholar: academic search engine
- ORCID: unique identifier for researchers
- Mendeley: reference manager
- ResearchGate: social network for researchers
- Academia.edu: publication sharing platform
- arXiv: preprint archive
- GitHub: development platform

These profiles help centralize my publications and increase my academic visibility.
