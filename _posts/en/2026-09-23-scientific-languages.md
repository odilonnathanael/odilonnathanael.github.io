## Python

- The most versatile
- Libraries: NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Pandas
- Widely used in AI, data science, physics

## MATLAB

- Common in engineering
- Integrated interface
- Specialized toolboxes

## Julia

- Modern and fast
- Syntax close to Python
- Designed for numerical computing

## R

- Specialized in statistics
- Excellent for data visualization
- Active community in social sciences

## Choosing a language

The choice depends on the application domain, available libraries and community.
